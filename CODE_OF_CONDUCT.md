# Code of conduct

## Our Pledge

`hackathon-finds` is a living notebook of hackathon advice, tips, and lived experience. It works because people feel safe sharing what actually happened to them — including the messy, embarrassing, or "we almost didn't submit in time" parts. We pledge to keep this a space where anyone, regardless of experience level, background, or how many hackathons they've won (or lost), feels welcome to contribute.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps this repo stay useful:

- Sharing advice or experiences honestly, including failures and lessons learned — not just highlight reels.
- Giving credit when an idea, tip, or story comes from someone else (a teammate, mentor, or another repo).
- Being respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Welcoming first-time contributors and first-time hackathon-goers with the same respect as veterans.
- Keeping feedback focused on the content, not the person.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in any form.
- Posting others' private information (emails, phone numbers, unpublished project details) without consent.
- Plagiarizing another person's or repo's content without attribution.
- Spammy, promotional, or off-topic submissions unrelated to hackathons or building.
- Dismissive or gatekeeping responses to beginners.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within the repo (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages) and in any other space where someone is representing this project — for example, sharing it on social media or at an event.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior can be reported by opening an issue tagged `conduct`, or by contacting the maintainer directly through their GitHub profile ([@ashilohfaith-debug](https://github.com/ashilohfaith-debug)). All reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion. Maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
